Product photography, release imagery and technical diagrams belong to their brands and original rights holders. Source URLs and colorways are recorded in the manifests linked above. These assets do not have a shared open reuse license.

封面目录含经过背景处理的素材；它们不是官方产品扫描、完整 360° 序列或测量依据。照片查看器展示离散角度；中底 3D 是解释性重建。

The cover assets include background-processed imagery. They are not official scans, complete 360-degree captures or measurement evidence. The photo viewer displays discrete angles, and the 3D midsoles are explanatory reconstructions.
