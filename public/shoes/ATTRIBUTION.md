Copyright belongs to Nike/Jordan, ANTA, Li-Ning/Way of Wade and adidas. No open reuse licence was found. The photographs are discrete product angles, not a continuous 360-degree capture or a scanned 3D model. ANTA regional style identifiers differ, as recorded in the manifest. Harden Vol.10 first launched in December 2025 and is listed as a 2026 season model.
